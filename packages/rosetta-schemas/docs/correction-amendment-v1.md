# Correction amendment and failure abstraction v1

Public proposal under #1625. This is a fixture-only governed extension composed from existing Observation, Conjecture, Evaluation and Receipt semantics, not a new Core kind, accepted Profile, private trust procedure or promotion authority. It consumes dependency-impact/revalidation (#1560), causal and bitemporal evidence (#1558), competing branch evidence (#141), federation/reputation evidence (#980/#1501), evidence independence (#1597), source identity, rights and write-admission owners. Those references require resolution by their owners.

The stable amendment reference names a proposed correction pattern; the content digest binds the exact bounded record. Original failure subject, repaired artifact and proposed mechanism are separate identities. A repaired artifact does not prove the mechanism. Applicability scope, constraint and context references and explicit uncertainty remain distinct from authentication and attributed receiver-local evaluation. A signed amendment can be quarantined, disputed or refuted. Corroboration requires evidence and a declared independence assessment, which this contract does not authenticate. Declared source lineages deduplicate aliases and derivatives; their number is not proof of independent support.

All twelve requested dispositions are representable. Local evaluations have an actor and context; challenge and refinement references preserve disagreement. Additive revisions retain exact predecessor, original subject and later observation; competing refinements remain separate. A declared scope narrowing can only remove named scopes or add constraints, with semantic applicability still decided by the responsible authority. Retraction and supersession preserve the previous artifact and refer to downstream impact. A receipt-only exchange can omit source references while carrying bounded evidence and a disclosure-policy reference. This structural omission is not a privacy guarantee: every emitted reference requires independently authorized disclosure. No receipt, amendment or evaluation grants local adoption, write permission, or deterministic promotion.

The focused tests specify and execute the ten adversarial cases in #1625: overgeneralization, malicious signed input, derivative multiplicity, independent convergence, unsupported corroboration, private narrowing, local-context mismatch, retraction, competing refinements, and attempted validator promotion. They are synthetic proposal vectors; acceptance and actual verification of signatures, local evidence, rights, and referenced artifacts remain separate.
