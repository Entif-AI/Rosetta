## Meaning that survives change

Rosetta is an open semantic, provenance, and interoperability protocol under active development. It explores how people, models, tools, organizations, and future cognitive systems can exchange claims while preserving enough identity, lineage, uncertainty, and authority context to understand what those claims mean after the implementation around them changes.

The Rosetta Stone is the governing image for a reason. It did not turn Greek, Demotic, and Egyptian hieroglyphs into one script. It preserved enough corresponding content for relationships among different representations to be recovered.

Rosetta aims for the same architectural property: **correspondence without compulsory sameness**.

That is why the project is closer to an interoperable Babel than a universal language. English can remain English. A model's latent state can remain model-specific. A scene graph can remain a scene graph. An external standard retains its own authority. Rosetta's job is to make the mappings, losses, sources, interpretations, and consequences explicit when those worlds meet.

## The semantic constitution

A central Rosetta discipline is refusing to collapse categories merely because one interface can render them similarly.

A source is not its summary. An observation is not an interpretation. An interpretation is not an evaluation. Repetition is not independent corroboration. A recommendation is not permission to act. A tool acknowledgement is not evidence that the physical world changed as intended.

Rosetta provides public machinery for representing those differences through stable artifacts, provenance, uncertainty, rights, receipts, lifecycle, and governed transformation.

A receipt is evidence of a recorded event or relationship. It is not a guarantee that a claim is true, that a source is reliable, or that an implementation satisfies a regulatory obligation.

## Public meaning, replaceable machinery

Rosetta is deliberately a narrow waist.

The public contract should expose enough semantics, schemas, mappings, validation, and conformance behavior that independent systems can interoperate honestly. An implementation does not need to publish every private routing, scoring, optimization, model-selection, learning, or deployment method that acts on Rosetta-shaped information.

The boundary is simple:

> Public Rosetta defines interoperable meaning. Implementations may innovate above it, but they may not quietly redefine it.

That distinction allows an ecosystem to remain open without demanding cognitive monoculture.

## Rosetta and the Unified Cognitive Architecture

Rosetta sits underneath a larger Entif research architecture.

**Bilqis** investigates whether semantic structure can be made cheaper to learn. **GNOSIS** studies dependency-aware reusable computation. **Akasha / Forget Me Not** governs persistent state and inheritance. **Codito** explores metacognitive mapping and orchestration. **OMoC** composes heterogeneous cognitive operators. **Commitment, Guard, and Tripwire** keep consideration, justified wager, admission to effect, and interruption distinct. **Swarm Gnosis** extends useful cognition across independently bounded participants. **IndraNet** supplies the relational fabric among those participants, called **Jewels** at the UCA layer, including a current specialization for qualified physical context.

Those names do not automatically become Rosetta Core types. The larger architecture can use Rosetta's public semantics without transferring authority over every project concept into Core.

## What the current protocol brings together

- **Meaning:** structured representations that make important distinctions explicit instead of relying on an opaque transcript.
- **Provenance:** links between sources, claims, transformations, and records of what occurred.
- **Ambiguity:** competing interpretations can remain live instead of being forced into one prematurely convenient answer.
- **Lifecycle:** corrections, supersession, derivation, invalidation, and revalidation can propagate without erasing historical evidence.
- **Interoperability:** Packs and Profiles can map external standards and domain contracts without pretending they are native Rosetta concepts.
- **Governance:** explicit boundaries around authority, rights, policy, disclosure, and consequential action.

## Why institutions should care

For enterprises and banks, the research addresses traceability across automated workflows and the human decisions that govern them. For governments, it concerns inspectable information exchange and accountable authority. For academia, it provides public representational contracts and research questions that can be tested independently. For investors and builders, the public repository exposes the distinction between the long-term thesis and the engineering evidence available today.

These are intended application areas, not claims of certified compliance, production deployments, or validated commercial outcomes.

## Evaluate the foundations

The repository contains working foundations alongside proposed designs. Evaluate individual packages, specifications, mappings, and conformance fixtures against your requirements; the research program is not a single finished product.
