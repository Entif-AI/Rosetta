Entif's projects are designed as interoperable layers, not as a collection of products all claiming authority over the same thing.

**Rosetta** is the open semantic and provenance constitution. **Bilqis** asks whether explicit semantic structure can make reusable cognition cheaper to learn. **Codito** studies the metacognitive and orchestration connective tissue that composes task-local cognition from heterogeneous operators. **IndraNet** connects independently bounded participants, or Jewels, and develops a concrete reference path through qualified physical context and Swarm Gnosis.

Around them, GNOSIS, Akasha, OMoC, SÍ SÉ SON, Commitment, Guard, Tripwire, Cognitive Tiles, Semantic Lattices, Tapestries, Generative Reality, Mission Control, and the Experience Compiler explore different costs and boundaries of the same cognitive episode.

The common rule is jurisdiction. A representation does not become truth by being convenient. A model does not become authority by being capable. A project does not become Rosetta Core because another project depends on it. Useful pieces meet through explicit contracts so that the architecture can change without making its meanings move underneath it.
